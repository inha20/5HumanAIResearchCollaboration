Master Handover Purpose 

This document serves as the master handover for the current state of the Human-AI Research Collaboration repository.

The repository originally focused on a case study of long-term Human-AI collaboration.

During subsequent analysis, a series of theory documents and evaluation tasks emerged.

The purpose of this handover is to summarize:

Current theory development Generated theory documents Generated evaluation tasks Recommended next actions 

This document should be treated as the primary entry point for future AI collaborators.

Repository Status 

The Human-AI Research Collaboration repository has evolved beyond a simple case study.

Current work explores whether the observations collected during the Minor Thesis research program can be organized into a broader theoretical framework.

The working candidate framework is:

Human-AI Research Continuity Theory (HARCT)

Core Observation 

The central observation emerging from the research program is:

Long-term Human-AI collaboration is fundamentally a problem of research continuity rather than intelligence alone.

The repository increasingly investigates mechanisms that preserve continuity across:
