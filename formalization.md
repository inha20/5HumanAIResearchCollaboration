ANTIGRAVITY Formalization Task Objective 

Evaluate whether the concepts developed within the Human-AI Research Continuity Theory (HARCT) framework can be formalized into a conceptual or mathematical model.

The goal is not to produce a rigorous mathematical theory immediately.

The goal is to determine whether the theory can move beyond descriptive observations toward a formal explanatory structure.

Assume the role of a theorist attempting to construct a formal model from the existing concepts.

Current Concepts 

The current framework includes:

Research Continuity Goal Preservation Context Transfer Reconstruction Cost Externalized Memory Distributed Research Memory Future-Self Collaboration Repository as Cognitive Infrastructure Human-AI Working Agreements 

The challenge is to determine whether these concepts can be represented as variables, relationships, processes, or systems.

Classify each major theory component.
