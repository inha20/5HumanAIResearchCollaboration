Theory Integration Task Objective 

Review all theory documents generated within the Human-AI Research Collaboration repository and determine whether they represent independent theories, supporting concepts, or alternative descriptions of the same underlying mechanism.

The goal is not to preserve every theory document.

The goal is to construct the most coherent and parsimonious theoretical framework possible.

Assume the role of a theorist attempting to compress a large collection of concepts into a smaller number of explanatory principles.
