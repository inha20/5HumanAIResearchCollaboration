# Human-AI Context Transfer Theory

## Abstract

Long-term Human-AI collaboration depends not only on memory preservation but also on the ability to transfer context across time, participants, systems, and repositories. This document proposes Human-AI Context Transfer Theory (HACTT), which argues that sustainable collaboration emerges when research context can move efficiently between humans, AI systems, artifacts, and future collaborators.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The Context Problem

Long-term research frequently spans multiple sessions, devices, repositories, and AI systems. During these transitions, information may remain available while usable context becomes inaccessible.

### 1.2 Central Claim

> Long-term Human-AI collaboration succeeds when research context can be transferred efficiently across time, participants, and systems.
