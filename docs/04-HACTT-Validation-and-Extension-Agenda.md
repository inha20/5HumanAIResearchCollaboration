# HACTT Validation and Extension Agenda

## Purpose

This document records the major open questions, validation tasks, and future extension opportunities for Human-AI Context Transfer Theory (HACTT).

HACTT was developed through observations gathered during the HARCT research program. Although the theory has reached a stable Version 1.0 structure, several aspects remain open to validation, measurement, and expansion.

The purpose of this document is not to defend HACTT unconditionally, but to identify the most important directions for future investigation.

---

# 1. Open Question: Minimum Context Unit

HACTT currently proposes that context consists of four components:

- Goal
- State
- History
- Next Action

## Critical Question

Why are these four components sufficient?

Why not three?

Why not five?

## Validation Tasks

### Component Removal Experiments

Evaluate project recoverability after removing:

- Goal
- State
- History
- Next Action

individually.

### Recovery Cost Comparison

Measure reconstruction effort under different combinations of context components.

### Continuation Success Analysis

Compare successful and failed project recoveries.

## Future Possibilities

Additional components may be identified in future versions of HACTT.

---

# 2. Open Question: Recoverability Measurement

HACTT defines Recoverability as:

> The ability to reconstruct sufficient context to continue a task, project, or research program.

## Critical Question

Can Recoverability be measured?

## Candidate Metrics

### Reconstruction Time

Time required to resume productive work.

### Token Cost

Number of tokens required for an AI system to recover project context.

### Artifact Count

Number of documents required for successful reconstruction.

### Recovery Success Rate

Probability of successfully continuing a project.

## Relationship to Token Economics

This question directly connects HACTT to Token Economics of Human-AI Research.

---

# 3. Open Question: Context Infrastructure

HACTT currently identifies repositories as a primary form of Context Infrastructure.

## Critical Question

Are repositories unique?

Or are they one example among many possible infrastructures?

## Candidate Alternatives

- Wikis
- Knowledge Bases
- Shared Memory Systems
- Research Databases
- Future AI Memory Architectures

## Future Research

Compare recoverability across different infrastructures.

---

# 4. Open Question: Generalization

HACTT was developed through observations gathered during HARCT development.

## Critical Question

Does HACTT generalize beyond HARCT?

## Candidate Domains

### Open Source Development

Developer-to-developer handover.

### Academic Research

Long-term collaborative research programs.

### Corporate Knowledge Transfer

Employee onboarding and project continuity.

### Multi-Agent AI Systems

AI-to-AI context transfer.

### Personal Knowledge Management

Future-Self collaboration through notes and reminders.

---

# 5. Research Predictions

## Prediction 1

Higher README quality reduces reconstruction time.

## Prediction 2

Loss of Goal, State, History, or Next Action increases reconstruction cost.

## Prediction 3

AI replacement does not necessarily reduce continuity if artifact quality remains high.

## Prediction 4

Repository-centered projects achieve higher recoverability than conversation-centered projects.

## Prediction 5

Higher handover quality reduces AI-to-AI transition cost.
